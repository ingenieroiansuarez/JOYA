# Domain models and events
Aggregate roots: Project, Quote, Order, PaymentIntent, InventoryLot, WorkOrder, CustodyTransfer, Certificate, Customer. Value objects: Money(amount decimal string,currency), Quantity(value,unit), RevisionRef(projectId,revisionId,digest), GeometryArtifact(kind,hash,unit,kernelVersion), Measurement(value,unit,source,confirmedAt), MarketPolicy(version,enabledCountries), Approval(actor,scope,revision,at). Distinguish estimated vs confirmed, natural vs synthetic, stock vs customer's property.

Event envelope: eventId UUID, eventType, schemaVersion, aggregateId, aggregateVersion, occurredAt UTC, correlationId, causationId, payload. Publish after DB commit. Consumer inbox unique (consumer,eventId), retry exponential jitter with max attempts and DLQ, never reorder same aggregate without detecting gaps. Re-drive authorized with evidence. No secrets or full customer PII. Commands carry expected version; 409 on stale version. Consumers for QuoteAccepted prepare order; PaymentConfirmed update allocation; InventoryReserved evaluate release; ProductionReleased notify workshop; InspectionPassed enables certificate; CertificateRevoked updates verify endpoint. Avoid event chaining that hides guard checks.
